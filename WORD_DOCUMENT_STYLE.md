# ✅ Word Document Style Applied! 📝

## 🎨 What Changed

The document now looks like a **real Word document** - no boxes, continuous flow!

### Before (Boxed Style):
- ❌ Each section in a card with borders and shadows
- ❌ Separated, boxed appearance
- ❌ Background colors on sections
- ❌ Heavy visual separation

### After (Word Document Style):
- ✅ **Continuous flow** like a real document
- ✅ **No boxes or borders** around sections
- ✅ **Clean white background** throughout
- ✅ **Proper typography** with justified text
- ✅ **Section headings** that flow naturally
- ✅ **Wide margins** (80px) like Word
- ✅ **Serif title** (Georgia) for document name
- ✅ **Sans-serif body** for readability

## 📐 Word-Like Features

### Typography:
- **Document title**: 28px Georgia (serif) - looks like Word
- **Section headings**: 20px sans-serif, bold
- **Body text**: 15px, line-height 1.8, justified
- **Proper spacing**: 16px between paragraphs

### Layout:
- **Centered**: Max 850px width (like Word's default)
- **Wide margins**: 80px horizontal padding
- **Top margin**: 60px vertical padding
- **Continuous**: Sections flow without boxes

### Collaborative Elements:
- **Agent tags**: Small, subtle badges next to section titles
- **Word count**: Minimal, monospace, unobtrusive
- **No visual clutter**: Only content matters

### Diagrams:
- **Inline**: Flow with the document
- **Light background**: #fafafa for subtle separation
- **Minimal borders**: Top/bottom lines only
